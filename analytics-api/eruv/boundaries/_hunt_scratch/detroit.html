Why now?

For more than forty years, the Detroit Eruv has been carefully maintained and faithfully relied upon.

But forty years is a long time.

Infrastructure ages, materials rot, usage takes its steady toll.

At the same time, the eruv now covers more areas, and faces more utility work, construction, and unannounced changes than ever before.

Nearly 1,000 families in Detroit benefit weekly from the eruv.

Fewer than 200 currently help sustain it.
