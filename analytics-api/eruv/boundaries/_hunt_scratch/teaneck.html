The Teaneck-Bergenfield Area Eruv consists of two connected Eruvin. The North Eruv is delineated in Blue, and the South Eruv in Red.
There are five (5) crossovers between the two sides.
It is generally not permissible to carry on any street designated as a border of the Eruv. Moreover, Route 4 crossovers must be utilized with care.
One must contact his/her Rabbi regarding the utilization of all border streets and all crossovers.
