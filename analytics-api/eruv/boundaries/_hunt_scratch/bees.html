    AREA SERVED

    ​

    As laid out by Rabbi Jachter, the Beachwood East eruv is bounded by Richmond Rd on the west, Cedar Rd on the north, the I-271 sound barrier on the east, and Shaker Blvd West on the south. It encompasses a significant number of houses, apartments and condominiums, Congregation Shaarey Tikvah, and Menorah Park and Montefiore. This increased accessibility has the potential to enhance the Sabbath experience of both residents and visitors.
